Fee Structure

Semester-Based & Parent-Friendly

At Practical Based Fast Learning School System, we follow a transparent, semester-based fee system designed to be affordable, flexible, and stress-free for parents.

Pre-1 Program Fee

Semester Fee: PKR 30,000

  • All worksheets (no textbooks required)
  • Activity-based learning materials
  • One educational or recreational study trip per semester
  • Technology-supported classroom learning

Grade 1 Onward (Step Levels)

Semester Fee: PKR 30,000

  • Complete worksheets and learning materials
  • One educational or enjoyment-based outing per semester
  • Practical, concept-based teaching approach
  • Technology-supported classroom learning

Flexible Installment Plans

Simple. Convenient. Parent-Friendly.

Parents can choose a payment plan that best suits them:

Option A: 2 Installments

  • First installment at admission
  • Second installment after 2 months

Option B: 3 Installments

  • Payment every 2 months

Option C: 4 Installments

  • Payment every 1.5 months

We believe in honest education pricing.

No Hidden Costs – Complete Transparency

  • No extra book fees
  • No uniform charges
  • No hidden or surprise costs

Everything your child needs for learning is already included in the semester fee.

Semester System Overview

Each academic year is divided into two semesters

One semester = 6 months

Ensures better evaluation, faster progress, and flexible learning
